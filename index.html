What counts as an AI fill?

One AI fill is one job the AI does for you:

  • filling in the report and quote from what you said (each time you say "Fill it in, Sayon" or "Stop Sayon", or tap Fill in the report)
  • filling in the agreement or treatment record when you tap Say it instead
  • reading a compliance plate from a photo
  • writing a photo's caption when you tap Describe

Listening while you talk is free and never counts. A typical inspection uses about 10 to 20 AI fills, and each paid plan gives every inspector 800 a month, about 40 inspections.

If you run out, nothing is lost. Your notes stay in the report and you can keep typing it by hand. AI fills come back on the 1st of each month, and upgrading gives you the bigger number straight away.

Your business owner looks after the plan. Ask them if you need more AI fills.
  • Paid through StripeSAYON never sees your card number.
  • Cancel any timeIn Manage. Your plan runs to the end of the month you've paid for.
  • Prices in Australian dollarsNo GST.
The fine print
  • The free trial is 14 days with 50 AI fills (about 3 to 5 inspections), and no card is needed.
  • Paying opens in your browser. Come back to SAYON afterwards and your plan shows here.
  • Business is billed yearly.
  • On Team and Business everyone on your team takes an inspector place, you included.
  • Adding places is charged straight away for the rest of what you've paid for, and only happens if the payment goes through.
  • For fewer places, the unused part comes off your next bill.
